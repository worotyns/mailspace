Local key management

1. My sending key

Your private key signs outgoing mail and reads your encrypted Sent copy. It must match the mailbox selected in From.

Import my existing key

Import my private key

Choose your exported ASCII-armored .asc file. Its public part is added automatically. Use the key for your sending mailbox address.

Use its existing passphrase. An unprotected key needs a new passphrase of at least 12 characters before storage.

2. Recipient keys

Their public key encrypts mail to them. You do not need their private key.

Add a recipient key

Add a recipient’s public key

Import is discovery, not proof of identity. Verify the full fingerprint independently before trusting an email binding.

3. Encrypt and sign your message

Choose your sending mailbox in From, enter the recipient, click Encrypt & sign, and unlock your own private key in the composer. Send encrypted submits only ciphertext. The recipient needs a compatible OpenPGP client and their own private key to read it.

Return to mail

Signing without encryption is not connected to sending yet. Existing encrypted sending already includes a signature.

Verify this email identity

The key and address are selected for you. Compare the full fingerprint with your own key file, or verify the recipient’s fingerprint through another trusted channel. Importing a public key alone does not verify its owner.

Advanced: generate a key, unlock here, or review trusted identities

Generate only if you do not already have a suitable key. Export your protected private key and revocation certificate as a backup.

Unlock or lock

Locked

Unlocked state is memory-only. Locking drops references; JavaScript cannot guarantee physical RAM erasure. Protect this origin, browser profile and exported backups. Clearing browser data removes the local vault.
